4shade — Support

4shade is a gallery of living shader art — hand-crafted, animated wallpapers for your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Contact

Common questions

How do I set a shader as my wallpaper?
Open a shader you like, tap the save button, and choose Wallpaper. It saves to Photos — then set it from Settings › Wallpaper (or long-press the Photo and choose “Use as Wallpaper”).

How do I add the Home Screen widget?
Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen, tap +, search for “4shade”, and add it. Long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget to pick a specific shader or let it rotate daily.

Why aren’t the widgets animated?
iOS renders Home Screen widgets as still images, so the widget shows a crisp frame of each shader rather than live motion. The full animation plays inside the app.

Do I need an account?
No. 4shade needs no sign-in and collects no data. The only permission it ever asks for is add-only Photos access, and only when you save a wallpaper.
