4shade — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

4shade collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests — everything runs on your device.

Photos

When you choose to save a shader as a wallpaper, 4shade requests add-only access to your photo library so it can write that one image. It never reads, browses, or uploads your photos. The saved image stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Data we collect

None. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers, no diagnostics.

Data we share

None. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared or sold.

Children

4shade is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
